# Privacy

NVR.io is intended for **personal use** — private contact and payment tracking.

## Principles

1. Collect only what you need for your own records.
2. Store consent version on each person record when applicable.
3. Request location permission with a clear purpose; do not collect silently.
4. Encrypt identity numbers at rest; restrict who can view them.
5. Do not put identity numbers in notifications, analytics, URLs, or browser localStorage.
6. Photos live in private application storage, not the device public gallery.

Data is saved on the NVR.io server / website, not as a permanent phone-local database.
